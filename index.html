About Broken Limb

Broken Limb has been making wood dart cases since 1999. Designed for assembled darts and spare parts, the cases combine CNC-machined hardwood with leather retainers and magnetic closures.

Cases are made in Virginia and finished with tung oil and furniture wax. Full-size and mini options are available, with optional engraving.
